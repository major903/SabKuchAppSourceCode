Delete Your Sabkuch Account

If you would like to delete your Sabkuch account and the data associated with it, please follow the steps below. You do not need to have the app installed to make this request.

How to request account deletion

Step 1: Send an email to support@sabkuchworld.com with the subject line "Delete My Account".

Step 2: Send the email from the email address or with the mobile number registered to your Sabkuch account, so we can verify your identity.

Step 3: We will confirm your request by email and delete your account within 30 days.

You can also request deletion directly from the app via Menu → Delete Account.

What data gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the data associated with it, including:

  • Your profile information (name, photo, contact details, date of birth)
  • Your login and authentication information
  • Your listings, preferences, and in-app activity linked to your account
  • Your location data associated with your account

What data we keep

Some data may be retained for a limited period where required for legitimate reasons such as security, fraud prevention, or legal/regulatory compliance (for example, transaction records). Retained data is no longer linked to an active account and is deleted once the retention requirement expires. For details, please see our Privacy Policy.
